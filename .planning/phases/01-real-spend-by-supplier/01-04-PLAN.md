## Phase Goal

**As a** business owner, **I want to** sign in and upload my invoice XML files, **so that** I see exact spend by supplier and month.

This plan is the ledger core that makes the numbers exact. It is pure code (no auth, no Drive) and runs in parallel with the Ajustes slice.

<objective>
Turn CFDI bytes into exact, normalized Invoice records and apply the Phase 1 spend rules: I adds the XML Total, E subtracts it, P adds zero (D-15), N/T are omitted and reported (D-07), concept-level reconciliation flags differences above $0.10 while the invoice still counts at its header Total (D-08).

Purpose: success criterion 4 (all 143 fixtures parse; exact decimals; reconcile within $0.10) and the golden totals behind success criterion 3.
Output: `src/domain/*`, `src/parsing/cfdi/*`, synthetic fixtures, three test suites.
</objective>
