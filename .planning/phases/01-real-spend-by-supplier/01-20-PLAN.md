## Phase Goal

**As a** business owner, **I want to** sign in and upload my invoice XML files, **so that** I see exact spend by supplier and month.

This plan makes the first visit and the edge states honest: a guided start instead of a dashboard of zeros, a clear message when the browser cannot store data, and a loading skeleton that never shows a false empty state. It was split out of Plan 01-13 on 2026-10-12 (former Task 3) to keep each plan within its context budget.

<objective>
Replace the Plan 01-12 placeholder states on `/` with the 01-UI-SPEC screens: the guided empty state with the privacy block (D-27, D-22, D-24), the storage-unavailable screen with "Reintentar" and no in-memory fallback (D-33), and one loading skeleton shared by `(app)/loading.tsx` and the client loading snapshot.

Purpose: the "never a dashboard of zeros" rule (D-27) and the D-33 error path, both in Spanish (DASH-09).
Output: `EmptyState`, `StorageUnavailable`, `DashboardSkeleton`, `(app)/loading.tsx`, the updated `DashboardScreen` and extended e2e coverage.
</objective>
