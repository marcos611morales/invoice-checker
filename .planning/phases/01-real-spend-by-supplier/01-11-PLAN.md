## Phase Goal

**As a** business owner, **I want to** sign in and upload my invoice XML files, **so that** I see exact spend by supplier and month.

This plan hardens the "sign in" half before any invoice data exists in the browser: a nonce CSP (the main defense for browser-stored fiscal data) and an authenticated browser test harness every later plan reuses. The 01-UI-SPEC shell, the sliding-session keep-alive, the server-side backstop for the browser wipe and the structural DAL test follow in Plan 01-18 (split out of this plan on 2026-10-12 to keep each plan within its context budget).
