This plan completes "by supplier and month" with the per-supplier monthly view and its dead ends. The Spanish root 404, the error boundary and the drag-and-drop upload (former Tasks 2 and 3, except the detail loading route and the anonymous detail redirect, which stay here) moved to Plan 01-22 on 2026-10-12 to keep each plan within its context budget.
