## Phase Goal

**As a** business owner, **I want to** sign in and upload my invoice XML files, **so that** I see exact spend by supplier and month.

This plan keeps that data private on a shared computer: it disappears at sign-out, when another account signs in, and when the user deletes it.

<objective>
Implement D-24 in its three client layers on top of the Plan 01-18 server backstop: wipe-first sign-out for "Cerrar sesión" and "Usar otra cuenta" with pending and failure states, the owner gate that deletes another account's invoices before anything renders, cross-tab sign-out, and the confirmed "Borrar facturas" dialog built on the shadcn `alert-dialog` approved at the Plan 01-19 legitimacy checkpoint.

Purpose: success criterion 5 ("signing out or the delete button removes them") and STOR-04's deletion rules.
Output: `wipeBrowserData`, `decideOwnerGate`, the updated sign-out buttons, `DeleteInvoicesDialog`, the `alert-dialog` primitive, unit tests and a Chromium wipe suite.
</objective>

- Gmail dot or plus aliases count as a different owner and wipe the data (the safe direction, RESEARCH Finding 7).
- If `signOut()` fails after a successful wipe, the user stays signed in with an empty store and can upload again (RESEARCH Finding 7 failure modes).
- A tab suspended by the browser may miss the sign-out message; the server redirects it to `/login` on its next navigation.
