## Phase Goal

**As a** business owner, **I want to** sign in and upload my invoice XML files, **so that** I see exact spend by supplier and month.

This plan lets the user drop XML files anywhere in the app and gives every remaining dead end a Spanish page. It was split out of Plan 01-16 on 2026-10-12 (former Task 3 and most of former Task 2) to keep each plan within its context budget.

Purpose: the drag half of success criterion 2, and Spanish dead ends (DASH-09).
Output: `DropOverlay` mounted in the shell, `src/app/not-found.tsx`, `src/app/(app)/error.tsx`, and extended e2e coverage.
</objective>
