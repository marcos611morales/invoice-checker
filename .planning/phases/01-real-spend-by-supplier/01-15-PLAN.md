**As a** business owner, **I want to** sign in and upload my invoice XML files, **so that** I see exact spend by supplier and month.

This plan delivers "see exact spend ... by month" on `/`, computed in the browser from the stored invoices. The supplier ranking, the top-10 chart, the year selector, the unreadable-records alert and the locked formatter outputs (former Tasks 2 and 3) moved to Plan 01-21 on 2026-10-12 to keep each plan within its context budget.
